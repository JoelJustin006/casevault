The Problem

Paper custody logs
don't survive cross-examination.

When digital evidence is seized — CCTV footage, hard drives, mobile dumps — proving it hasn't been altered between the crime scene and the courtroom is a massive legal hurdle. Today's chain-of-custody relies on paper forms and human trust. A single missing signature, an unlogged copy, or a switched file invalidates the evidence. CaseVault replaces that trust with mathematics.
